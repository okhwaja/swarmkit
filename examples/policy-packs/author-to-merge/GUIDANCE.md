# Author-to-merge workflow

This pack coordinates an explicitly authorized change; installation does not grant
provider permissions. Adapt its provider checks and bounded skill to your harness.
An additional gate before authoring is necessary when creation was not authorized.

The manager publishes this graph and its delivery commitment atomically. The
producer binds the provider object/revision before completing. It does not wait
for review to complete authoring. The release stage supplies a structured brief,
records the authenticated human answer, and links that decision to the continuation.
If release is declined, the manager explicitly cancels or revises remaining work
and disposes the commitment; do not run a declined continuation.

The continuing worker requires the current selected choice before requesting
review, commenting, pushing, or merging. Your offered choice must clearly include
those actions if intended. An external review signal is not the owner's decision.
Approval and comment disposition are independent. Resolve all feedback through
fixes, answers, or agreed dispositions; never just bulk-close threads. Wait for
reviewer acknowledgement where required. Ordinary scoped repairs need no new
question to the owner; scope changes, exceptions, and contradictory authority do.

Use the named skill for one bounded inspect/repair/verify interval. Rebind the
commitment revision after authorized repairs; do not reuse old-revision evidence
or grants. The adapter authenticates reviewer identity and determines whether
approval survives that revision. Missing approval is an external wait. Use
commitment observe for a trusted current provider read-back, then commitment wait
to release your attempt and exit. Fetch the current version with commitment show
before mutations. After uncertainty about wait completion, retrieve its state;
do not reclaim or republish using the retired owner.

The harness's trusted issuer supplies exact-action grants under the recorded
human scope; it must not expose unrestricted issuance to the worker. Trusted
named checks attest approval, addressed comments, required CI, and mergeability
on the current revision. Bind the merge effect to that grant and enforce the
same preconditions at the provider. Reconcile EXECUTING/UNKNOWN effects before
another action. Provider credentials, webhook verification, retries, and remote
revision rules belong to the adapter, not this policy text.

A merge queue acknowledgement is not landing. Only a current trusted
change-landed observation can satisfy the commitment. Record pending or rejected
observations without claiming success; a rejected/closed-unmerged change needs
manager replanning. The producer and continuation use opaque provider revisions;
no Git requirement is implied. After landing, record final task evidence and
complete the continuation. Context and responsibility survive every agent exit.
